# NorthBridge Insurance — Underwriting Guidelines FAQ

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Age Requirement**: Applicants must be 18 years or older to hold a policy in their own name. Minors must be listed as dependents.
- **Auto Risk Tiering**: Auto applicants with 3 or more at-fault accidents within the past 3 years are ineligible for standard coverage and are referred to high-risk underwriting.
- **Home Inspections**: Homes older than 40 years must pass a certified electrical and roof inspection before coverage can be bound.
- **Required Underwriting Review Documents**:
  - Auto Underwriting: Certified Copy of Motor Vehicle Record (MVR) for the past 3 years.
  - Home Underwriting: Signed Roof Inspection Certificate, Electrical Hazard Report (for homes over 40 years old), and prior loss history report.

## Pricing & Premium Factors
- **Auto Premium Factors**: Auto premiums are determined by driving records, vehicle model, age, location, and estimated annual mileage.
- **Home Premium Factors**: Home premiums are calculated based on construction materials, roof age, geographic location, and proximity to fire hydrants.
- **Multi-Policy Discounts**: Bundling auto and home policies qualifies the policyholder for a 12% discount on both policies.

## Application Process
- **Review Period**: Standard digital applications are reviewed within 3 business days.
- **Pending Inspections**: Applications requiring inspection remain in a pending status until inspections are completed and signed off.
- **Backdating Prohibited**: Binder coverage cannot be backdated. All coverage starts exactly at 12:01 AM on the approved effective date.
