# NorthBridge Insurance — Claims Processing Manual

## Filing a Claim Procedure
- **Reporting Deadline**: Policyholders must report any claim to NorthBridge Insurance within 30 days of the incident. Claims reported after this 30-day window are subject to additional review, verification, and potential denial.
- **Claim Reference Number**: Upon submission, a unique 8-digit claim reference number (e.g., CL-100234) is generated instantly for tracking and audit.
- **Required Claim Documentation Checklist**:
  - Valid Government-Issued Photo ID (Driver's License, Passport).
  - Proof of active NorthBridge Policy (Policy Card or digital PDF copy).
  - Detailed written description of the incident, including date, time, and precise location.
  - Official Police Report (mandatory for auto accidents, theft, vandalism, and property burglary).
  - High-resolution photographs showing the property damage or vehicle damage from multiple angles.
  - Certified repair estimates from an authorized contractor or mechanic.
  - Receipts for temporary emergency repairs or alternative living arrangements.

## Claim Evaluation Timeline
- **Auto Adjuster Assignment**: Auto claims trigger immediate adjuster assignments, and initial contact with the claimant is guaranteed within 2 business days.
- **Home Inspection Timeline**: On-site home inspections for property damage must be scheduled and conducted within 5 business days of filing.
- **Standard Resolution**: Standard, fully documented claims are resolved within 15 business days.
- **Complex Claims**: Claims involving values greater than $25,000 are designated as complex and may take up to 45 business days to resolve.

## Payout Guidelines
- **Payment Speed**: Approved payouts are processed and issued within 5 business days of the final adjuster sign-off.
- **Distribution Channels**: Payouts default to direct deposit (ACH bank transfer). Mailed checks are sent if requested, adding 3-5 shipping days.
- **Deductible Adjustment**: The applicable policy deductible (e.g., $500 for auto, $1,000 for home) is automatically subtracted from the final approved claim payout.

## Disputes & Appeals
- **Appeals Window**: Policyholders can appeal any denied claim within 60 days of the denial notice.
- **Senior Adjuster Review**: Appeals are assigned to senior adjusters who were not involved in the original claim decision.


## Claims Dispute Resolution & Appeals
- **Dispute Escalation Window**: Policyholders must submit formal claim appeals in writing within 180 days of receiving the initial claim decision or denial notice.
- **Independent Adjuster Review**: If a dispute regarding damage valuation exceeds $10,000, either party may request an independent inspection by a certified appraisal adjuster. The cost of appraisal is split equally between the insurer and the policyholder.
- **Subrogation Recovery Operations**:
  - Subrogation processes apply when a third party is determined to be at fault for causing damages.
  - The Claims department must seek recovery of all claim payments and the policyholder's deductible from the negligent party's insurance carrier.
  - Policyholder deductibles must be reimbursed on a pro-rata basis within 30 days of receiving subrogation recoveries.

## Total Loss Computations & Vehicle Salvage
- **Total Loss Threshold (TLT)**: A vehicle is declared a constructive total loss if the estimated cost of repairs plus the salvage value exceeds 75% of the vehicle's Actual Cash Value (ACV) immediately prior to the loss.
- **Actual Cash Value (ACV) Appraisals**: Valuation is determined using local market comparable sales data, vehicle condition adjustment indexes, and depreciation schedules based on mileage.
- **Owner Retention Options**: If the policyholder retains the salvage vehicle, the salvage value (as determined by certified salvage auctions) is deducted from the final total loss settlement payout.

## Special Investigations Unit (SIU) Fraud Triggers
- **Red Flag Anomaly Triggers**:
  1. Claims filed within 30 days of policy inception or reinstatement from lapse.
  2. Inconsistent vehicle damage patterns compared to the accident scene description.
  3. Lack of supporting document files (e.g., missing police reports for vehicle theft).
  4. Multiple claims filed by the same policyholder for similar perils within a 24-month period.
- **Investigation Timeline**: SIU investigations must be completed within 60 days of referral. A formal report must be generated detailing findings before claim settlement authorization is granted or denied.
