# NorthBridge Insurance — Billing & Payments FAQ

## Payment Cycles & Options
- **Billing Frequency**: Policyholders can select monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or annual billing cycles.
- **Pay-in-Full Benefit**: Choosing the annual pay-in-full option applies a 5% discount to the total annual premium.
- **Payment Channels**: Accepted methods include automated clearing house (ACH) bank transfer, credit/debit cards, and paper check.

## Missed Payments & Grace Periods
- **Billing Grace Period**: A 10-day grace period is provided for all missed or late payments. No late fees are applied during this period.
- **Late Fees**: A $25 late fee is applied to the account on day 11 after the payment due date.
- **Policy Lapsation**: Policies are automatically cancelled due to non-payment (lapsed) after 30 days of outstanding balance. Reinstatement requires full payment of the balance plus a $50 fee.

## Cancellations & Refunds
- **Pro-Rata Refunds**: Cancellations receive a pro-rated refund of any unused premium, processed within 10 business days.
- **Cancellation Penalty**: A $50 short-rate fee is applied if the policyholder cancels the policy mid-term (before renewal). No fee applies at the renewal date.
