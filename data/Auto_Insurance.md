## Personal Auto Insurance Policy — Detailed Clauses


Clause 1 — Liability Coverage (Bodily Injury and Property Damage) The Insurer will pay damages for bodily injury or property damage for which any insured becomes legally responsible because of a covered auto accident. Limits are typically expressed as split limits such as $100,000 per person / $300,000 per accident for bodily injury and $50,000 per accident for property damage (100/300/50), or as a combined single limit. The Insurer also provides a legal defense at its own expense, in addition to the limits of liability. Defense costs do not reduce the coverage available for damages.

Clause 2 — Collision Coverage Collision coverage pays for direct and accidental loss to the covered auto caused by its impact with another vehicle or object, or by the vehicle overturning, regardless of fault. Payment is made at the actual cash value (ACV) or cost to repair, whichever is less, minus the deductible shown on the Declarations Page (commonly ₹25000, ₹50000, or $1,000). Collision coverage requires a separately elected premium and applies only to listed vehicles.

Clause 3 — Comprehensive (Other Than Collision) Coverage Comprehensive coverage pays for loss to the covered auto from causes other than collision, including fire, theft, vandalism, falling objects, hail, flood, windstorm, riot, contact with animals, and glass breakage. A separate, often lower, deductible may apply. Glass-only claims may be settled with no deductible where state law or the endorsement provides.

Clause 4 — Uninsured Motorist (UM) Coverage If an insured suffers bodily injury caused by an owner or operator of an uninsured motor vehicle, the Insurer will pay compensatory damages the insured is legally entitled to recover. This includes hit-and-run accidents where the responsible party cannot be identified. Limits mirror the Bodily Injury liability limits unless the insured has rejected or reduced them in writing where permitted.

Clause 5 — Underinsured Motorist (UIM) Coverage When the at-fault driver carries liability limits lower than the insured's damages, UIM coverage pays the difference up to the UIM limit. This coverage applies only after the at-fault party's limits have been exhausted and is subject to state-specific offset and stacking rules.

Clause 6 — Medical Payments Coverage (MedPay) MedPay covers reasonable and necessary medical and funeral expenses incurred by the insured and passengers as a result of a covered accident, regardless of fault, up to the elected limit (commonly $1,000 to $10,000 per person). Coverage generally applies to expenses incurred within a set period, typically three years from the date of accident.

Clause 7 — Personal Injury Protection (PIP) In no-fault jurisdictions, PIP pays medical expenses, a percentage of lost wages, essential services, and death benefits regardless of who caused the accident. Benefit amounts, deductibles, and coordination with health insurance are governed by state statute and elected coverage tiers.

Clause 8 — Rental Reimbursement Coverage When the covered auto is disabled by a covered collision or comprehensive loss, this coverage reimburses rental costs up to a daily maximum (for example, $40 per day) and an aggregate maximum (for example, $1,200 per loss). Coverage begins after the loss is reported and ends when the vehicle is repaired, replaced, or the maximum is reached.

Clause 9 — Roadside Assistance / Towing and Labor This coverage pays for towing to the nearest qualified repair facility and for on-site labor such as jump-starts, tire changes, fuel delivery, and lockout service, up to the per-disablement limit shown on the Declarations Page. Labor must be performed at the place of disablement.

Clause 10 — Definition of Insured Persons An "insured" includes the Named Insured and resident spouse, family members related by blood, marriage, or adoption who reside in the household, and any person using the covered auto with permission within the scope of that permission. Coverage for permissive users is subject to the same terms and limits.

Clause 11 — Covered Autos and Newly Acquired Vehicles Covered autos include those listed on the Declarations Page, trailers owned by the insured, and newly acquired vehicles. A newly acquired vehicle receives automatic coverage broadest to the coverage the insured already carries, provided the Insurer is notified within 14 days (30 days for additional vehicles in some states).

Clause 12 — Policy Territory Coverage applies to accidents and losses occurring within the United States, its territories and possessions, Puerto Rico, and Canada. It also applies during transportation between their ports. Coverage does not extend to Mexico unless a specific Mexico endorsement is purchased.

Clause 13 — Deductible Application The applicable deductible is subtracted from each collision or comprehensive loss payment. Where a single event causes both collision and comprehensive damage, only one deductible — generally the higher — may apply, subject to state rules. Deductibles may be waived where the loss is caused by an identified, insured at-fault third party and subrogation is successful.

Clause 14 — Exclusion: Intentional Acts No coverage applies to bodily injury or property damage caused intentionally by, or at the direction of, an insured. This exclusion applies even if the actual injury or damage is different or greater than expected.

Clause 15 — Exclusion: Racing and Speed Contests The policy does not cover loss occurring while the covered auto is being used in, or in practice or preparation for, any prearranged or organized racing, speed, or demolition contest, or stunt activity.

Clause 16 — Exclusion: Commercial and Livery Use Losses occurring while the vehicle is used to carry persons or property for a fee — including delivery services and ride-share driving during passenger pickup or transport — are excluded unless a business-use or ride-share endorsement has been added.

Clause 17 — Exclusion: Non-Owned and Furnished Vehicles Coverage does not extend to vehicles furnished or available for the regular use of an insured but not declared on the policy, nor to vehicles owned by the insured that are not listed. This prevents insuring one vehicle while regularly driving another.

Clause 18 — Total Loss Settlement When repair costs plus salvage value exceed the vehicle's actual cash value, or exceed a state-defined total-loss threshold, the vehicle is declared a total loss. The Insurer pays the ACV determined by market valuation, less any applicable deductible, and takes ownership of the salvage.

Clause 19 — Subrogation Rights After paying a claim, the Insurer succeeds to the insured's right to recover from any responsible third party. The insured must do nothing to impair these rights and must cooperate in recovery efforts. Amounts recovered are applied first to reimburse the Insurer, with any deductible portion returned to the insured on a pro-rata basis.

Clause 20 — Duties After an Accident or Loss The insured must promptly notify the Company, cooperate in the investigation, submit to examination under oath if requested, provide proof of loss, protect the vehicle from further damage, and notify police when required (such as theft or hit-and-run). Failure to comply may reduce or void coverage for that loss.

Clause 21 — Cancellation and Nonrenewal The insured may cancel at any time by returning the policy or giving notice of the future cancellation date; earned premium is calculated pro rata. The Insurer may cancel for nonpayment of premium, material misrepresentation, or suspension/revocation of the driver's license of an insured, subject to statutory notice periods (commonly 10 days for nonpayment and 30–45 days for other reasons). Nonrenewal requires advance written notice as prescribed by state law.

Clause 22 — Anti-Theft and Safety Discounts Premium reductions apply for approved anti-theft devices, factory or aftermarket alarm systems, vehicle recovery systems, airbags, anti-lock brakes, and safe-driver telematics enrollment. Eligibility must be verified and documented; discounts are applied at issuance or renewal.

Clause 23 — Two or More Autos / Other Insurance If the policy covers two or more autos, the limits apply separately to each. When other collectible insurance applies to a loss, this policy pays only its share. For non-owned autos, this policy is excess over any other applicable coverage.
