# NorthBridge Insurance — Customer Support SOP

## Agent Escalation Flow
- **Standard Inquiries**: Agents should answer billing, basic auto/home coverage, and claim filing questions using the primary knowledge base.
- **Level 2 Escalate**: If a customer queries underwriting exceptions, disputed claim denials, or complex multi-vehicle premium rates:
  - The agent must escalate the ticket to a Supervisor.
  - Required ticket fields for escalation: Customer CRM ID, Policy Number, Claim Reference Number (if applicable), and Escalation Reason.
  - Escalated tickets are resolved within 24 hours.

## CRM and Customer Records Lookup SOP
- **Verification Rule**: Before answering customer billing details or claim payout statuses, the agent must check the customer's CRM record.
- **Key Customer Records**:
  - CRM ID: `CRM-101` (Customer: Alice Smith, Policy: `POL-AUTO-501`, Status: Active)
  - CRM ID: `CRM-102` (Customer: Bob Jones, Policy: `POL-HOME-902`, Status: Inactive)
  - CRM ID: `CRM-103` (Customer: Charlie Davis, Policy: `POL-LIFE-303`, Status: Active)

## Communication Templates
- **Standard Email Draft**: When drafting customer replies, agents must follow this structure:
  - "Dear [Customer Name], thank you for contacting NorthBridge Insurance..."
  - Summarize policy details or claim status explicitly.
  - Cite the relevant section (e.g., Policy Handbook Section 2.1).
  - Conclude with standard support footer.
- **Supervised Action Gate**: Any CRM update, ticket escalation, or outbound email draft must pass through supervisor human-in-the-loop (HITL) approval prior to final transmission.
