# NorthBridge Assurance — Vehicle Insurance Policy Handbook (Session 2026-2027)

## Clause 1: Liability Coverage
The insurer pays damages for bodily injury or property damage for which an insured becomes legally responsible because of a covered auto accident. Liability coverage is intended to protect an insured when a covered accident results in a legal obligation to compensate another person for injuries or damage to their property.
- **Split Limits**: Typical split limits include ₹1,00,000 per person / ₹3,00,000 per accident for bodily injury and ₹50,000 per accident for property damage (100/300/50), or a combined single limit.
- **Per-Person Limit**: Maximum available for bodily injury to one individual.
- **Per-Accident Limit**: Maximum available for all bodily injuries arising from the same covered accident.
- **Property Damage Limit**: Applies separately to covered property damage.
- **Legal Defense**: The insurer provides a legal defense at its own expense when the insured is involved in a covered liability matter. Defense costs do not reduce the coverage available for damages.
- **Exclusions**: Excludes intentional acts, racing or speed contests, and commercial or livery uses unless covered by endorsement.

## Clause 2: Collision Coverage
Collision pays for direct and accidental loss caused by impact with another vehicle or object, or overturning, regardless of fault.
- **Payment Basis**: Payment is based on actual cash value (ACV) or cost to repair, whichever is less, minus the Declarations Page deductible.
- **Vehicle Listing**: Collision requires separately elected premium and applies only to listed vehicles.
- **Covered Collision Events**: Impacts another vehicle, impacts an object, overturns as a result of a covered collision event, or sustains direct/accidental physical damage resulting from a covered collision.
- **Fault**: Collision applies regardless of fault.

## Clause 3: Comprehensive Coverage
Comprehensive (Other Than Collision) covers listed causes including fire, theft, vandalism, falling objects, hail, flood, windstorm, riot, animal contact, and glass breakage.
- **Deductibles**: A separate deductible applies. Glass-only claims may have no deductible where state law or an endorsement provides.
- **Scope**: Direct and accidental physical loss resulting from listed non-collision causes.

## Clause 4: UM and UIM Coverage
- **Uninsured Motorist (UM)**: Applies to bodily injury caused by an uninsured motor vehicle, including qualifying hit-and-run situations.
- **Underinsured Motorist (UIM)**: Pays the difference when the at-fault driver's liability limits are lower than the insured's damages, after those limits are exhausted.

## Clause 5: Auto Medical Payments (MedPay)
MedPay covers reasonable and necessary medical and funeral expenses for the insured and passengers resulting from a covered accident, regardless of fault.
- **Common Range**: ₹94,491 to ₹9,44,905 per person.
- **Coverage Period**: Applies to expenses incurred within 3 years from the accident date.

## Clause 6: Personal Injury Protection (PIP)
In no-fault jurisdictions, PIP pays medical expenses, a percentage of lost wages, essential services, and death benefits regardless of fault.
- Governed by state statute and elected coverage tiers.

## Clause 7: Rental and Roadside Assistance
- **Rental Reimbursement**: Applies when covered auto is disabled by a covered collision or comprehensive loss. Pays ₹3,780 per day, up to 30 days per incident (maximum ₹1,13,388 per incident).
- **Roadside Assistance**: Pays for towing to the nearest qualified facility and specified on-site labor up to the per-disablement limit.

## Clause 8: Covered Persons and Autos
- **Insured Persons**: Named Insured, resident spouse, qualifying resident family members, and permissive users within the scope of permission.
- **Covered Autos**: Listed vehicles, qualifying trailers, and newly acquired vehicles subject to notification period.

## Clause 9: Exclusions and Deductibles
- **Exclusions**: Intentional acts, organized racing/speed contests, specified commercial/livery use. Non-owned vehicles regularly furnished to an insured and unlisted owned vehicles are excluded.
- **Deductibles**: Applicable collision or comprehensive deductible is subtracted from loss payment.

## Clause 10: Claims, Settlement and Duties
- **Total Losses**: Insurer pays ACV determined by market valuation, less applicable deductible, and takes salvage ownership.
- **Subrogation**: Subrogation allows the insurer, after paying a covered claim, to pursue recovery from a responsible third party.
- **Insured Duties**: Promptly notify the insurer of loss, cooperate with investigation, provide proof of loss, protect vehicle from further damage, notify police when required.
